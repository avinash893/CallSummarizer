# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Singers, instrumentalists, and ensemble jam groups** practicing songs, scales, and collaborative sessions remotely from desktop or mobile browsers.
- **Remote meeting participants** capturing live transcripts, AI summaries, and Google Calendar action items inside the same room session.

## Product Purpose

SargamJam Studio combines low-latency WebRTC video/audio jamming with real-time pitch watching, acoustic Tanpura synthesis, BPM metronome, cloud stem separation, multi-track DAW mixing, and automated meeting notes in one browser workspace.

## Positioning

Unlike generic video-call tools or single-player tuners, SargamJam synchronizes room-wide musical Scale, acoustic Tanpura accompaniment, BPM tempo, and 10%-tolerance vocal pitch bars across live collaborators with server-side YouTube/audio stem separation.

## Operating Context

Used in darkened practice rooms, home studios, and live call sessions on desktop (1280px+) and mobile (390px) viewports with live microphone input, dual vocal/backing audio playback, and Google OAuth calendar permissions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Live room video/audio via WebRTC (`Jam Stage`), interactive pitch/lyrics matcher (`Pitch & Lyrics`), and offline-rendered 2-channel FX mixer + AI Coach (`Studio DAW`).
- Cloud stem separation (`Demucs` + `Whisper` + `yt-dlp`) hosted on Modal Serverless with persistent cache volume.
- Google OAuth2 + Cloudflare Turnstile gating on session creation and individual Google Calendar event insertion.

## Brand Commitments

- **Product Name:** SargamJam Studio.
- **Mode:** Operate — clarity, scanability, tabular precision, and standard platform controls outrank decorative ornament.

## Product Principles

1. Put live tuning, scale state, and playback controls within reach without modal interruption.
2. Keep every metric (`Hz`, `cents`, `BPM`, `Error %`, timers) honest, tabular, and immediately readable.
3. Preserve WCAG AA/AAA legibility on dark surfaces during long practice and call sessions.
